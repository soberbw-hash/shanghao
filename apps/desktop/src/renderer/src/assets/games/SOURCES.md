# Game artwork sources

The game marks are bundled locally so room rendering never depends on a live network
request. They are used only to identify a game detected on the user's own computer.

- Steam capsule artwork: official Steam store CDN (`capsule_231x87.jpg`) for Kingdom
  Rush, Slay the Spire, Delta Force, Counter-Strike 2, Dota 2, Apex Legends, PUBG,
  Overwatch 2, NARAKA, GTA V, Rainbow Six Siege, Monster Hunter: World, Black Myth:
  Wukong, Lost Castle 2, Elden Ring, It Takes Two, Palworld, Overcooked 2, and Red
  Dead Redemption 2.
- Minecraft: `minecraft.net` official site icon.
- League of Legends and VALORANT: Riot Games official site/press assets.
- Genshin Impact and Honkai: Star Rail: HoYoverse official site icons.
- Fortnite: Epic Games official launcher logo asset.
- Stardew Valley monitor scene: official gameplay screenshot from the
  [Stardew Valley Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/413150/Stardew_Valley/),
  published by ConcernedApe
  ([source image](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/413150/ss_a3ddf22cda3bd722df77dbdd58dbec393906b654.1920x1080.jpg)).
- League of Legends monitor scene: official in-game jungle artwork from Riot Games'
  [How to Play page](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/how-to-play/)
  ([source image](https://cmsassets.rgpub.io/sanity/images/dsfx7636/news/7c790e73c1d817e57c174d4c4cdceb87d061e5b6-2560x1440.png?accountingTag=LoL)).
- Delta Force monitor scene: official gameplay screenshot from the
  [Delta Force Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2507950/Delta_Force/),
  published by Team Jade / TiMi Studio Group
  ([source image](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2507950/ss_e25ef30c6cdff5c5ff48e4ac0e7856ec4ebbb10e.1920x1080.jpg)).
- The three source screenshots are cropped to 16:10, resized to 768 x 480, adjusted for
  tiny-screen readability, and encoded as WebP. No logo or text was added.
- Future scene artwork must follow `GAME_MONITOR_ART_GUIDE.md`, including its tiny-screen
  composition rules and two-pass visual review.

All game names, logos, and artwork remain the property of their respective owners.

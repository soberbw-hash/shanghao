# Third-party animal-call audio

ShangHao uses these short local recordings for character reminder calls. Playback is limited
to roughly two seconds and processed at runtime with level normalization, light compression,
and short fades. The bundled WAV files are level-normalized excerpts of the original Ogg
recordings.
